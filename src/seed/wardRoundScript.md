# Ward-round demo script — SYNTHETIC, non-PHI

**This is a fixture, not a record.** Every name, date, drug, dose and result below
is invented for demonstration. No part of it describes a real person, a real
admission, or a real conversation. It exists so the voice-capture path can be
exercised without recording a real ward round — WA is an all-party-consent
jurisdiction (`STRATEGY.md` §8) and a real round is a private conversation.

Read aloud at a normal ward pace. Two speakers is realistic but one is enough for
the demo; the extractor is speaker-agnostic.

---

## Bed 4 — "Margaret Nolan", 71, fictional

**Registrar:** Right, bed four, Margaret Nolan, day three post admission for the
community acquired pneumonia.

**Registrar:** She is allergic to penicillin, that is a documented anaphylaxis, so
we have kept her off the beta lactams entirely.

**Intern:** Her CRP came back at 142 this morning, down from 210 yesterday.

**Registrar:** Good. The white cell count is 11.2, also trending down.

**Registrar:** I have started her on doxycycline 100 mg BD orally, and we will
continue that for a further five days.

**Registrar:** Keep the paracetamol 1 g QID PRN charted for the pleuritic pain.

**Intern:** The chest x-ray showed persistent right basal consolidation, reported
this morning.

**Registrar:** She is also intolerant of metoclopramide, she gets a dystonic
reaction, so use ondansetron if she needs an antiemetic.

**Registrar:** Expected discharge Thursday if the oxygen requirement stays off.
